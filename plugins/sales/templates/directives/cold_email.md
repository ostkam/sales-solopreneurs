# ✉️ Cold email: Seen, So, Small yes

> **One email = one observation, one reason, one small offer.**
> Email is the second channel, a few days after LinkedIn, or the first one when the
> person is not active on LinkedIn.

---

## The shape

| Part | What it does | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Seen** | One specific thing you saw about *them*, usually the signal | Checkable, never a compliment |
| **So** | Why that usually matters | One sentence, "usually / often", no numbers you cannot prove |
| **Small yes** | Something small and free you make for them, plus a clean exit | Say what it is and how long it takes them |
| **Easy reply** | A yes/no line | "Reply yes and I'll make it." |

Then your first name. Nothing else.

---

## Example *(made up)*

> **Subject:** Your bookkeeping hire
>
> Hi Anna,
>
> I saw Northwind is hiring a second bookkeeper.
>
> That usually means invoices and receipts are growing faster than the team can handle,
> and the backlog lands on the owner at month end.
>
> I could record a 3 minute video showing how one of your invoice types could be booked
> automatically, using only what your public site shows. If it's not useful, ignore this
> and that's the end of it.
>
> Reply yes and I'll make it.
>
> Kim

---

## ✅ Do and ❌ avoid

| ✅ Do | ❌ Avoid |
|---|---|
| Subject of 2 to 4 plain words about them | Hype, question marks, "Quick question" |
| Under 110 words | Links or attachments in the first email |
| One idea only | Two observations in one email |
| A clean exit | "I hope this finds you well", calendar links |
| One follow-up after 4 to 6 days that adds something new | A third and fourth "just bumping this" |

**Sending:** use only verified emails, warm up a separate sending domain first, and never
send cold email from your main domain.
