# 👥 Personas

> **Who we talk to inside a company that fits.**
> One section per persona, usually 1 to 3: the one who signs, the one who feels the pain
> first, and sometimes the one who uses the result. Claude drafts them, you correct them.

**Status legend:** ✅ found or confirmed · 🟡 inferred, please check · ⬜ missing

The **titles** row matters most: when you import people from LinkedIn, each person is
tagged with the persona whose title words match their job title.

<details>
<summary><b>What each parameter means</b> (click to open)</summary>

| Parameter | The question it answers | Example |
|---|---|---|
| titles | Which job title words do we search and match? (comma-separated) | founder, ceo, managing director, owner |
| department and seniority | Where do they sit? | Management, owner or director level |
| decision role | Do they sign, feel the pain first, or use the result? | Signs the budget |
| pains | Their top 3 problems your offer touches, in their own words | "Month-end eats my weekends" |
| desired result | What does "better" look like for them? | Close the books without them |
| objections | What will they say to say no? | "We tried software, nobody used it" |
| trusted proof | What convinces them? | A peer's story, a free sample, numbers |
| channel and language | Where do they answer, and in which language? | LinkedIn, German |

To add a persona, copy the whole `## Persona: …` section and give it a new name.
