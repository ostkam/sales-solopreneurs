<details>
<summary><b>What each parameter means</b> (click to open)</summary>

| Parameter | The question it answers | Example |
|---|---|---|
| offer | What do you sell, in words a buyer understands? | "Bookkeeping automation for small agencies" |
| outcome | What result does the customer get? | "Month-end closing in 2 days instead of 10" |
| proof | What can you show? | Named customers, case studies, numbers |
| alternatives | What do they do today instead of buying from you? | A spreadsheet, a part-time employee, an agency |
| industries | Where are your best customers? (1 to 3) | Marketing agencies, architecture firms |
| customer type | What kind of company? | Agency, manufacturer, SaaS, clinic, law firm |
| company size | How many employees does a good customer have? | 10 to 200 |
| regions | Which countries and languages can you serve? | UK and Ireland, English |
| deal size | What does a first purchase cost? | 3,000 to 10,000 |
| triggers | What happens just before they need you? These become your **signals**. | They hire a bookkeeper; a new CFO starts |
| disqualifiers | Who do you never contact? | Over 500 staff, competitors, public sector |

`Source` = the web page a fact came from, `owner` (you said it), or `inferred`.
